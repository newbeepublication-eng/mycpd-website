This page explains how to delete your **myCPD** account (the myCPD – Professional Logbook app on Google Play and the App Store, and app.mycpd.ie) and what happens to your data. The app is provided by Newbee Hub Ltd.

## Delete your account in the app {#in-app}

1. Sign in to myCPD on your phone or at [app.mycpd.ie](https://app.mycpd.ie).
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Before you delete, you can use **Download My Data** on the same page to keep a copy of your CPD record.

## Delete some of your data, and keep your account {#some-data}

You can delete individual items yourself at any time, without deleting your account:

- **CPD entries:** open the entry in your CPD Journal and choose **Delete**.
- **Notes and meeting minutes:** open the note or minutes and choose **Delete**.
- **Uploaded files:** remove the file from the entry it belongs to.

Deleted items are removed from your account straight away. To ask us to delete a specific type of data for you, email **info@newbeepublication.com**.

## Ask us to delete your account {#by-email}

If you can't sign in, email **info@newbeepublication.com** from the email address on your account, with the subject **"Delete my myCPD account"**. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, as required by GDPR.

## What is deleted {#deleted}

- Your account and profile (name, email, profession and settings)
- All CPD entries, reflections and hours
- Uploaded certificates and evidence files
- Supervision logs, reports and supervision agreements
- Meeting minutes and notes
- Reminders, calendar items and app activity linked to your account

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## What is kept {#kept}

- A short deletion log containing only your email address and the date of deletion, kept for regulatory compliance. It contains no CPD data.
- If you paid for a subscription, payment and invoice records held by our payment processor, Stripe, are kept by Stripe for as long as the law requires. Cancel any active subscription before deleting your account so you are not charged again.

Automated backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted data also disappears from backups over time.
